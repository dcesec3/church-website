OUR STORY

44 Years of
Fithful Community

Deliverance Church was established in 1982 as part of the larger Deliverance church movement in Kenya, grounded equally in scripture, service and one another. What began as a small prayer group meeting in a home has grown a vibrant congregation of believers passionate about serving God and the community.

We believe faith is lived alongside one another. Our doors are open to everyone at every stage of their spiritual journey.
